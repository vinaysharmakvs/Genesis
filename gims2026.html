Have Any Questions?

What is GIMS?

GIMS, or the Genesis Ignited Minds Scholarship Test, is designed to help students understand their academic capabilities in real time. It combines objective and subjective assessment with guidance that supports academic and overall development.

What is the structure of the GIMS Scholarship Test?

The test includes objective and subjective sections, with dedicated support for CBSE and ICSE students. The assessment looks at accuracy, reasoning, explanation quality and readiness.

When and where will the exam be conducted?

Exam 1 is scheduled for 4 October 2026 and Exam 2 for 25 October 2026. Students can choose the available test mode during registration. Paper discussion is held offline on the nearest Sunday.

What is the registration fee for GIMS?

The early registration fee is Rs 149. The regular fee is Rs 199. The final amount and selected mode are shown during registration.

What scholarship opportunities are available?

Students can earn up to 100% scholarship. The total scholarship pool is worth Rs 1 lakh, with scholarships declared soon after the exam.
